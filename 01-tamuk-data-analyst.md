# Data Analyst Intern

**Organization:** Texas A&M University–Kingsville
**Program:** REFLEX-ME (Research, Education and Financial Literacy Excellence for Minority Engagement), through LEAPS at Laredo College
**Dates:** May 2022 – Aug 2022

## Summary

Research program analyzing how monthly COVID-19 case counts related to unemployment rates in five countries (Brazil, France, Germany, India, and the United States) from January 2020 to May 2022.

## Tools

- Python
- Excel

## What I did

1. Collected monthly COVID-19 case counts and unemployment rates for five countries.
2. Cleaned and organized the data in Excel and Python.
3. Compared case trends against unemployment trends for each country.
4. Measured how closely cases and unemployment moved together.
5. Looked at timing differences between unemployment changes and each country's case waves.
6. Documented the results and what they did and did not show.

## Later work

My brother, Marco Ortiz, and I later recreated the project as an interactive dashboard: [COVID-19-UER-Analytics](https://github.com/summer-waves/COVID-19-UER-Analytics).
