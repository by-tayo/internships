# Aerospace Engineer Intern

**Organization:** Texas A&M University–Kingsville
**Program:** NASA MUREP PEMS, through LEAPS at Laredo College
**Dates:** May 2022 – Aug 2022

## Summary

Computational aerodynamics research on whether a UAV (unmanned aerial vehicle) could fly in Mars's thin atmosphere, followed by a wing redesign to improve lift.

## Tools

- Autodesk Fusion 360
- SolidWorks
- NASA FoilSim III
- Airfoil Tools
- Excel

## What I did

1. Studied flight conditions on Mars, including its low atmospheric density and gravity, against Earth as a baseline.
2. Modeled how those conditions change the forces on an aircraft.
3. Redesigned the UAV's wing geometry in Fusion 360 and SolidWorks, expanding the wingspan and wing area.
4. Simulated airfoil lift and drag in NASA FoilSim III and Airfoil Tools.
5. Compared the lift of the redesigned wing against the original.
6. Tracked the force calculations in Excel spreadsheets.
7. Co-authored a technical report on the findings.
8. Presented the results to faculty.

## Skills

Aerodynamic modeling · CAD design · Simulation · Technical writing · Presenting
