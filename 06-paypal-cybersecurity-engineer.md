## What I did

1. Reviewed research papers and articles on post-quantum cryptography, including Shor's algorithm and the "harvest now, decrypt later" threat.
2. Traced how encryption evolved, from RSA to NIST post-quantum standards.
3. Compared ML-DSA-44, ML-DSA-65, and ML-DSA-87 by strength, standards fit, and use case.
4. Analyzed certificates and keys in PEM, DER, PKCS#7, PKCS#8, PKCS#12, and JKS formats.
5. Worked with certificate authorities and root chains, including reissuing certificates.
6. Tested post-quantum certificates across environments with TLS and mTLS handshakes.
7. Built a dual/hybrid certificate server for testing.
8. Measured handshake performance and latency, calculating the mean, 95th percentile, minimum, and maximum.
9. Wrote automation scripts to support server updates.
10. Learned certificate lifecycle management: issuing, renewing, and reissuing certificates over time.
